Course Curriculum

Module 01 Asana Mechanics

Intelligent Asana Practice & Alignment

Deconstruct essential postural families, including standing flows, twists, backbends, inversions, and restorative holds. Learn intelligent sequencing axioms that prioritize foundational stability, structural balance, and alignment to safely safeguard vulnerable joints.

Key Focus: Sthiram & Sukham Alignment Framework
Module 02 Pranayama & Locks

Pranayama, Bandhas & Breath Regulation

Study the profound physiological intersection of your breath and nervous system. Master structural practices including ocean-sounded Ujjayi, calming Viloma patterns, and Nadi Shodhana, while activating internal core locks to guide your inner energy safely.

Key Focus: Vagus Nerve Stimulation & Mental Stillness
Module 03 Yoga Humanities

History of Yoga, Wisdom Texts & Meditation

Explore foundational philosophical texts including timeless self-inquiry in the Upanishads, the pathways of the Bhagavad Gita, and the practical guidance of Patanjali’s Eight Limbs. Integrate this historical wisdom seamlessly into modern daily life and mindfulness.

Key Focus: Mind Cultivation & Traditional Mantra Philosophy
Module 04 Art of Teaching

Teaching Methodology & Class Architecture

Learn how to architect clear, compelling class maps from the opening grounding to optimal peak-pose work. Build confidence in precise verbal cueing, room management, smart prop modifications, and holding a welcoming, trauma-informed space.

Key Focus: Authentic Presence & Professional Business Ethics
